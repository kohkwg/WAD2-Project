## Limitations

- Travel times are estimates (distance with a detour factor) unless the OneMap server is configured.
- Sample listings have approximate hours; scores depend on the chosen weights, which are a design choice.
- Eight hand-made cases are not a benchmark; they show behaviour on situations the plan calls out.
